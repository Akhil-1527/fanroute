          Numbers labelled Qloo come from the Qloo Taste AI API and describe aggregate taste affinity: where an artist's audience over-indexes. They are not ticket sales, not a count of people, and say nothing about any individual. Ranks, the route, the room order and the pitch text are Fanroute's own interpretation of those numbers. Treat every lead here as a reason to send an email, not as a promise. Of what you type, only the artist name and home city are sent to Qloo. Chat messages go to a Workers AI model, and Fanroute does not store them.
